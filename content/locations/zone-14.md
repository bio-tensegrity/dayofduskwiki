<div style="text-align: center;">
Zone-14 is arguably one of the most dangerous places in the entire map, shrouded in mystery and lethal radiation. Originally a research bunker and mining site for the AEGIS Corporation, it was abandoned after the devastation of the second and third resource wars. The area is now infamous for its clusters of radiant green Fedorlite, a valuable and hazardous resource.

### Zone 14 History
Zone-14 was used to research the effects of the Rockstar volcano and mine resources unearthed by volcanic activity. After the resource wars, nuclear strikes left the area irradiated and uninhabitable. The discovery of Fedorlite brought miners and scavengers back, but the zone was eventually sealed due to deadly radiation. ARD occasionally ventures in to maintain the last reactor.

### Fedorlite Discovery
Fedorlite, a glowing green ore, is abundant in Zone-14. The zone was equipped with machinery to create batteries for AEGIS. After years of exploitation, the area was drained and closed, only to be reopened with new mining methods. Today, only those with proper gear survive long enough to extract resources.

### Modern Zone 14
Now abandoned, Zone-14 is visited only by desperate miners, ARD, and the occasional wastelander. The proximity to the Rockstar volcano ensures a steady supply of Fedorlite. ARD pays handsomely for broken batteries and rare resources, but the risks are extreme.

### Entering Zone-14
To enter, blow a hole in the wall using the bomb icon. Inside, mine Fedorlite, gather battery parts, and use the refinery to create valuable batteries. Escape with your haul and sell to ARD—if you survive the radiation and hazards.
